Nifty Seller Lite 2.56.0_PHASE4_STRATEGY_LAB

Phase-4 goal
- Add an on-demand Strategy Lab around the protected plans already produced by TradePlan.
- Keep the canonical One Brain, execution gate, broker usage and thresholds untouched.

Added
1. Protected payoff lab
   - CE SELL / PE SELL / IRON CONDOR / CE BUY / PE BUY when the existing plan is available.
   - Expiry payoff curve using the plan's protected legs and executable-style bid/ask entry assumptions.
   - Max profit, max loss, breakevens and rupees-per-lot context.
2. Combined Greeks
   - Net Delta, Gamma, Theta and Vega from the current option-chain rows.
   - Coverage indicator and per-leg signed Greek table.
3. Spot / IV / Time What-If
   - User-controlled spot move, IV-point move and time-forward sliders.
   - Local Delta/Gamma/Vega/Theta approximation only; clearly marked as not a forecast.
   - Approximate P&L change in points and rupees per lot.
4. Strategy comparison
   - Side-by-side quality, entry credit/debit, max profit/loss, breakevens,
     net Delta/Theta and liquidity floor.
5. UI integration
   - New persistent panel immediately after Best Strategy + Strike Value Table.
   - Nothing is calculated until the Strategy Lab panel is opened.

Golden Rule protection
- Zero new broker/API calls.
- Zero history/disk reads.
- No One Brain score, weight or threshold changes.
- No TradePlan or execution-gate mutation.
- Core decision.py, simple_brain.py, trade_plan.py, option_intelligence.py,
  barrier_map.py, big_player.py, execution_guard.py, dhan_gateway.py and
  snapshot_service.py remain byte-for-byte unchanged from 2.55.0.

Notes
- Expiry payoff excludes brokerage, taxes, slippage and fill drift.
- Pre-expiry What-If uses local Greeks and can diverge on large spot/IV/time moves.
- Strategy Lab is analysis/display only and never becomes a second strategy brain.
