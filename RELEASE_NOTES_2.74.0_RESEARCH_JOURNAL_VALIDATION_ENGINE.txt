Nifty Seller Lite v2.74.0 — Research Journal Validation Engine

PURPOSE
Turn the existing paper journal into a validation system for both the canonical One Brain and the shadow Market Intelligence layer, without changing any strategy calculation, score, threshold inside those engines, protected strike plan, or broker-data pipeline.

WHAT CHANGED
1. Separate validation lanes
   - ONE BRAIN
   - MARKET INTELLIGENCE
   Historical P&L/win-rate summaries are no longer mixed across the two lanes.

2. One Brain paper trigger
   - Simple Brain final action must be a concrete protected setup.
   - Direction strength >= 54/100.
   - Entry readiness >= 62/100.
   - Existing protected plan, live quotes/candles/option chain, risk budget and entry-window guard must still be ENTRY READY.
   - Real one-trade/day discipline does not suppress research sampling; the live trading engine itself remains unchanged.

3. Market Intelligence paper trigger
   Priority A: Institutional Window OPEN/STRONG, 6/6 gates, data-safety eligible.
   Priority B: Pressure Integrity VERIFIED/REALIZED plus active ATTACK/BREAK support (or barrier attack >=65).
   Seller research mapping: BULLISH -> PE SELL; BEARISH -> CE SELL.
   Liquidity Magnet alone never creates a paper trade.

4. Research capacity
   - 25 One Brain paper samples/day.
   - 25 Market Intelligence paper samples/day.
   - 5-minute per-lane cooldown.
   - Same strategy may have overlapping paper samples because the purpose is entry-timing/model validation, not portfolio simulation.

5. Frozen entry evidence
   Stores One-Brain direction/readiness/action, Institutional Window state/score/gates, Pressure Integrity state/score/fake-real quality, Move Attack/barrier state, Liquidity Magnet bias/scores/confidence, path clearance and OB-vs-MI alignment.

6. Outcome review
   Existing conservative executable pricing is retained:
   - Short entry uses bid; hedge/long entry uses ask.
   - Short exit uses ask; hedge/long exit uses bid.
   - SELL spread P&L uses entry credit minus current close debit.
   - BUY spread P&L uses current close value minus entry debit.
   Closed trades now also store 5m/15m/30m directional outcomes, duration, MFE/MAE and evidence-based likely profit/loss factors.
   Causation is never claimed as proven.

7. Journal reliability
   - Decision Journal read/update/write is one locked atomic transaction.
   - Railway paper monitor merges later outcome fields for closed trades while refusing stale OPEN payloads that would reopen them.
   - Decision and paper CSV downloads are separate.
