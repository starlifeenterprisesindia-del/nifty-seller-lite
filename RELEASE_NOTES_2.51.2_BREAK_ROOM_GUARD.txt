Nifty Seller Lite 2.51.2_BREAK_ROOM_GUARD
=========================================

Purpose
-------
Fix the live false-break entry pattern observed on 29-09-2026 around 13:39 without
adding API calls, history reads, blocking work, or extra heavy computation.

What changed
------------
1. Armed-break room guard (DOWN)
   - The old/frozen support trigger may still break exactly as designed.
   - Before CE SELL can become TAKE NOW, the app now checks the already-computed
     current nearest support zone.
   - If a fresh support is too close, the state becomes HOLDING / NEAR and final
     action remains WAIT instead of selling directly into support.

2. Armed-break room guard (UP)
   - Symmetric protection for PE SELL / bullish continuation.
   - A broken old resistance does not release TAKE NOW when a fresh resistance
     is immediately ahead.

3. Room threshold
   - Minimum usable room = max(8 NIFTY points, 0.60 x current 3m ATR14).
   - This is deliberately small and local: it blocks obvious chase/no-room cases
     without forcing a full extra 3-minute candle wait on clean breaks.

4. 15:15 broker/index freeze
   - Intentionally unchanged and ignored as requested.
   - No change was made to late-session reference/freeze handling or Top-9 logic.

Golden Rule / performance
-------------------------
- No new broker/API request.
- No new disk/history read.
- No new network call.
- No change to One-Brain weights.
- No extra indicator calculation.
- Only a few comparisons against the barrier map already present in the snapshot.

Regression evidence
-------------------
The 29-Sep false entry pattern was: old support ~22,646 broke, while a fresh support
zone ~22,620-22,632 was already directly below the completed 3m close. The old
version treated the frozen trigger as BROKEN and released CE SELL. The new guard
keeps WAIT because usable room to the fresh support is below the threshold.
