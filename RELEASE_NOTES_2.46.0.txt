paper journal and Railway persistent-volume behaviour remain intact.

Changes
- Best CE SELL, PE SELL, CE BUY and PE BUY candidates now include Future Brain
  alignment while retaining barrier, delta, liquidity, live bid/ask and risk gates.
- SELL pair rank is reward-aware: quality 35%, credit/risk 25%, net decay 25%
  and Future Brain alignment 15%. Premium equality is not an objective.
- Pair audit now includes net theta edge and a labelled theta-only 15-minute estimate.
- Iron Condor jointly compares protected CE x PE wing combinations instead of simply
  joining two independently selected wings.
- Future UP widens CE risk room and may allow a closer PE decay wing; Future DOWN
  applies the reverse; RANGE prefers balanced spot risk room.
- Extremely low reward spreads/condors are blocked as SAFE BUT LOW REWARD.
- Directional buy spreads keep meaningful delta response and reject extremely weak
  maximum-profit/debit combinations.
- After Future Brain is calculated, the protected trade plan is re-ranked and the
  Execution Guard is recalculated from that exact plan.
- Spot-to-Premium remains connected to R1/R2/S1/S2 barriers, barrier strength and
  break pressure, VIX remaining move, short-horizon speed and opposite-barrier stop.

Safety
- Future Brain cannot bypass Current Brain, Common Gate, live bid/ask, barrier,
  liquidity, hedge, max-risk or market-session blocks.
- Calculations are estimates for paper testing, not guaranteed profit.
