Nifty Seller Lite v2.73.1 — Journal Visibility + Opening Capture

Why this patch exists
- The app had two different journal lanes but the UI label "Auto Shadow Journal ON" made them look like one feature.
- Decision Journal already recorded WAIT/READY/ENTRY observations, while Paper Trade Journal only opened a simulated trade after all protected-entry gates passed.
- A full live day can therefore have many decision rows but zero new paper trades when One Brain remains WAIT. That is expected, not a journal failure.
- The decision-journal recording window began at 09:30, leaving the important 09:15–09:30 opening period out of the journal.

Changes
1. Decision Journal now records from 09:15 to 15:00 IST.
2. Sidebar toggle renamed to "Auto Paper Trades ON". Decision Journal is automatic and independent of this toggle.
3. Sidebar now states that Decision Journal is AUTO and shows its recording window.
4. Journal panel renamed "Decision + Paper Journal" and clearly separates Decision rows from gate-passed Paper Trades.
5. Journal health can show LOCAL RECORDING while Railway history is still warming, avoiding a false impression that the journal is dead.
6. 0 paper trades is explicitly explained as expected when One Brain final action remains WAIT.
7. Decision-file writes retain atomic replacement and now use a dedicated lock path for safer multi-session access.

Unchanged
- One Brain decision logic
- Execution Guard
- Trade Plan
- Position Guardian
- Market Intelligence / Pressure Integrity / Institutional Window / Liquidity Magnet
- Smart Entry Advisor
- Dhan/API request count
- Paper-entry qualification thresholds

Validation
