Nifty Seller Lite v2.25.0 — Big Player Activity

- Added one responsive Big Player Activity screen for phone and laptop.
- Uses the same authoritative One-Brain snapshot: time-normalized NIFTY futures
  volume, futures price/OI setup, ATM option flow, Top-7 and barrier reaction.
- Shows BUYING / SELLING / MIXED, 0–100 activity, 2/3 persistence, reversal risk,
  time window, futures setup and compact reasons.
- Colour severity becomes stronger as participation rises; extreme selling is red,
  extreme buying is dark green and conflicts/absorption remain clearly labelled.
- Stores only a bounded same-day direction/score journal. It does not identify a
  specific institution, place orders or create a second decision brain.
- Confirmed 2/3 activity has a maximum six-point bounded adjustment inside the
  existing calculate_final_decision function.
