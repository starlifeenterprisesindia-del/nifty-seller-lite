Nifty Seller Lite 2.45.0 — Future Decision Workspace

WHAT CHANGED

1. Current Brain now describes the market as it is now. It no longer owns the
   future strategy decision.
2. Future Brain calculates separate 5-minute and 15-minute UP, DOWN and RANGE
   paths, transition/reversal state, confirmation and invalidation.
3. A Common Final Gate now joins Current context, Future direction, live feeds,
   protected strike availability, alignment and risk budget. Only this gate can
   mark an entry as allowed.
4. Best Strategy + Strike ranking is filtered by Future Brain direction:
   UP -> PE SELL / CE BUY, DOWN -> CE SELL / PE BUY, RANGE -> IRON CONDOR.
5. The strategy table now shows a compatible protected package and preferred
   premium entry zone, while clearly remaining WAIT/reference-only when blocked.
6. Auto Shadow Journal can no longer create an experimental trade from a WAIT
   candidate. A paper trade requires the same Common Final Gate action.
7. Recorded observations now store Future Brain and Common Gate fields so later
   outcomes can calibrate the forecast without silently overriding fresh data.
8. Full evidence download now streams a gzip file from Railway. It avoids the
   former 12 MB JSON/base64 limit and lowers peak RAM usage on small plans.
9. Public snapshot export includes common_decision for auditability.

IMPORTANT SAFETY MEANING

- Forecast percentages are path/evidence estimates, not guaranteed profit odds.
- Historical hit rate appears only after enough matching observed outcomes.
- WAIT always overrides the strategy ranking.
- The app remains advisory and does not place broker orders.
