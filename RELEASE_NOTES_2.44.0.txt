Nifty Seller Lite v2.44.0 — Two-Brain Learning Engine
=====================================================

What changed
------------
- Brain 1 remains the authoritative Current Market state (UP/DOWN/RANGE).
- Brain 2 is a separate leading forecast for the next 5 and 15 minutes.
- The Transition Gate explicitly distinguishes continuation, reversal watch,
  confirmed paper-test reversal, range/compression and no-edge WAIT.
- Future Brain uses RSI slope, MACD-histogram change, recent 1m momentum,
  barrier room, option bias, Top-9 breadth and bounded big-player evidence.
- Percentages are labelled FORECAST SCORE until enough matching outcomes exist.
  They are never described as guaranteed win probability.
- Railway outcomes now retain a compact regime key and label the actual NIFTY
  move 5/15/30 minutes later. Matching history calibrates at most 25% of the
  live forecast, so old data cannot overpower fresh evidence.
- Historical status is explicit: INSUFFICIENT DATA, EARLY ESTIMATE, MODERATE
  HISTORY or STRONGER HISTORICAL BASE.
- Auto Shadow Journal floor is 55. Blocked alignment and Future-Brain WAIT
  checks remain rejected observations; they no longer contaminate paper P&L.
- A 3m-confirmed forecast reversal may be observed as an experimental paper
  test while the slower 15m state remains visible as context. It never places
  a broker order and does not relax the real execution guard.
- WAIT candidates compare direction-compatible BUY and SELL structures. Range
  candidates can select an Iron Condor instead of forcing directional selling.
- Railway DAY_MEMORY_SLIM keeps one-minute outcome continuity but stores only
  important nearby option rows and canonical decision/price/flow evidence.
  Repeated raw Greek copies and distant option rows are omitted in production.
- Future Brain is included in the app, public snapshot, quick report, complete
  diagnostic report and support bundle.

Important interpretation
------------------------
- Current strength = present-state evidence, not a future probability.
- Future score = next-path estimate; it becomes historically calibrated only
  after enough matching observed outcomes.
- Entry quality and strategy P&L remain separate from market-direction labels.
- Existing Railway volume records and old paper journal entries are preserved.
- No automatic broker order or profit guarantee is added.

Deployment
----------
Upload this cumulative package over the repository root and redeploy Railway.
Keep one Railway replica, a persistent volume and DAY_MEMORY_ENABLED=1. The
included railway.toml enables DAY_MEMORY_SLIM=1.
