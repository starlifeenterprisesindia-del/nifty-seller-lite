Nifty Seller Lite 2.55.0_PHASE3_REPLAY_REVIEW

Phase-3 goal
- Turn already-recorded Railway evidence into an explicit, on-demand One Brain replay/review screen.
- No broker call, no One Brain score/weight change, no threshold auto-tuning.

Added
1. One Brain Replay + Review panel
   - Manual Load/Refresh button; nothing is read until requested.
   - Replay step slider through recorded session samples.
   - Recorded Action, Direction, Regime, Entry Readiness, Big Player and Option Bias.
2. Historical replay chart
   - Recorded NIFTY 1-minute candles only.
   - Selected replay point overlays R1/R2/S1/S2 and CE/PE OI walls.
   - Recorded One Brain signal and Big Player markers.
   - Full-screen button.
3. Barrier + Heavy Money timeline
   - Barrier ranges, strength, break pressure and state.
   - CE/PE wall strikes and wall migration.
4. Outcome review
   - 5m/15m/30m recorded spot outcomes and label coverage.
   - Action/regime counts and descriptive median move statistics.
   - No win-rate or P&L claim.
5. Event review
   - Recorded BARRIER / 3m REACTION / APP AI / AI TRACKER events.
6. Railway endpoint
   - /day-memory-replay reads only persistent SQLite evidence.
   - SQL projects replay fields only and does not copy the saved option-row arrays.

Golden Rule protection
- Core decision.py, simple_brain.py, trade_plan.py, option_intelligence.py,
  barrier_map.py, big_player.py, execution_guard.py are byte-for-byte unchanged vs 2.54.0.
- dhan_gateway.py and snapshot_service.py are unchanged.
- Replay work is explicit/on-demand and outside the live decision path.
