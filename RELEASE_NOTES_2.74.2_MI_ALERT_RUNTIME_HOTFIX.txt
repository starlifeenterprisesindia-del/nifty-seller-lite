Nifty Seller Lite v2.74.2 — MI Alert Runtime Hotfix

Issue
- During a live Market Intelligence alert cycle, ui/market_intelligence.py referenced `institutional` inside process_market_intelligence_alerts() without defining it in that function scope.
- This could stay hidden while there were no eligible MI alerts, then crash a Streamlit rerun as soon as an alert path was reached.

Fix
- Bind `institutional` from the already-computed snapshot.metadata["market_intelligence"]["institutional_window"] inside process_market_intelligence_alerts().
- No new broker/API request.
- No calculation, score, threshold, barrier, One Brain, Pressure Integrity, Liquidity Magnet, Institutional Window, Smart Entry Advisor, journal, or P&L logic changed.
