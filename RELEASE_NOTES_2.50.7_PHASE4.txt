Nifty Seller Lite 2.50.7_PHASE4_DIAGNOSTICS_REPLAY

Phase 4 follows the Golden Rule: performance diagnostics and replay review are
read-only/on-demand. No new broker/API calls are added to the One-Brain critical
decision path and no strategy threshold is auto-tuned.

Changes
- Added a Performance Diagnostics panel using existing snapshot timing metadata.
  It shows pipeline/build/finalize timing, refresh headroom/load, slowest stages,
  and feed freshness/status without rebuilding a snapshot.
- Added explicit snapshot-mode visibility so UI reruns can be distinguished from
  fresh snapshot builds.
- Polished Post-market Replay with selectable horizon/move threshold, observed
  episode summary, move magnitude, WAIT-vs-signal context, and Big Player timing.
- Added median same-direction Big Player observation lag and <=3m/<=5m counts.
- Replay remains manual/read-only and does not modify recorded samples, live scores,
  strategy decisions, or Big Player thresholds.

Deferred
- IV Delta remains deferred until a near-zero-cost cached/asynchronous design is
  available. It is not added to the One-Brain calculation path.
