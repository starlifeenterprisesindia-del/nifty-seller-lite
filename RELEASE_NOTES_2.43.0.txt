Nifty Seller Lite v2.43.0 — One-Brain Workspace
================================================

This is a cumulative update. It includes the pending v2.42.4 audit-integrity
fixes; do not upload an older patch first.

What changed
------------
1. One canonical forecast contract now drives the main view and timeframe view.
   Next 5–15 minute UP/DOWN/RANGE evidence paths always total 100.
2. Strategy-direction compatibility is enforced:
   UP -> CE BUY / PE SELL; DOWN -> PE BUY / CE SELL;
   RANGE -> protected IRON CONDOR; mixed -> WAIT.
3. Iron Condor recognizes a stable balanced range without requiring the literal
   15-minute label RANGE. Tight compression remains a breakout-risk warning.
4. The strategy table is mobile-friendly. Advanced decay/value/pair columns are
   collapsed. The best compatible setup shows current package premium,
   preferred limit zone and do-not-cross price.
5. Every sell setup remains protected. Short+hedge should be placed as one
   package; if staged, hedge-first. Naked short is never advised.
6. Auto Shadow Journal records 45+ experimental candidates instead of deleting
   every near-miss. 60+ score/confidence plus safety alignment is QUALIFIED.
   Exact blocker/warning is saved and displayed.
7. Historical paper win-rate/average P&L appears only after 20 closed samples;
   before that the app says insufficient samples. Records never add a second
   live-direction vote and do not guarantee future profit.
8. UI is compact: one status bar, main forecast, levels, strategy and journal
   status stay visible. Detailed/timeframe/data/tools are collapsed.
9. Checks & Downloads are consolidated at the bottom.
10. Railway OOM protection: one Uvicorn worker, concurrency cap, reduced malloc
    arenas, minute-snapshot cleanup, one generated report retained at a time,
    and a 12 MB API export ceiling.

Important
