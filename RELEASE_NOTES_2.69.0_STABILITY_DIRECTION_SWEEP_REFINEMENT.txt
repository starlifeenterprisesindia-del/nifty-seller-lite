Nifty Seller Lite — v2.69.0_STABILITY_DIRECTION_SWEEP_REFINEMENT

Purpose
-------
Consolidated live-shadow refinement based on 07-Oct-2026 screen recording, Market Intelligence Test Pack,
Support Bundle, 3m/15m charts and option-chain evidence. This is not a new strategy and adds no broker call.

1) One-Brain evidence stability / score flip-flop
- Fixed a regime-hint fallback bug where max(UP, DOWN) >= threshold could select the regime side even when
  the regime-side score itself was weak. The hinted side must now be credible and not materially weaker.
- Options evidence no longer has a READY -> zero-weight denominator cliff when the composite score is
  temporarily zero but current 1m/3m option-flow windows are already READY.
- The fallback uses CURRENT snapshot windows only, at deliberately reduced quality. No stale option score is
  carried forward. Truly missing evidence remains NO VOTE.
- Option and Participation weights scale with current evidence quality instead of binary all-or-nothing weight.
- User-facing Direction/Entry scores use bounded display hysteresis to reduce noisy 10-15 point visual jumps.
  Raw scores still drive the operational action logic and are recorded separately.

2) Early direction refinement
- 15m remains dominant structural context, not a short-horizon veto.
- Fast-family direction uses independent families only: Structure, Futures, Options Flow, Barriers/Walls,
  Heavyweight Breadth and Momentum Acceleration. Trend is deliberately excluded from this fast vote.
- A fast direction may override a MIXED precaution view only when the consensus is broad, the signed edge is
  meaningful, pressure is actively accelerating and structure supports it (or expansion is exceptionally high).
- Added context labels: WITH TREND, BREAKOUT WATCH, EARLY PRESSURE, REVERSAL WATCH, COUNTERTREND IMPULSE.
- Countertrend/reversal/early-pressure warnings stay precautionary; they cannot become CONDITIONS MET merely
  from short-timeframe acceleration.

3) Liquidity sweep / stop-cascade refinement
- A first close through a liquidity zone is now BREACH — PENDING ACCEPTANCE, not instant continuation.
- The exact breached pool is locked for up to five minutes so recalculated targets cannot move the goalpost.
- CONTINUATION FAVORED requires follow-through acceptance on completed 1m closes.
- Quick reclaim of the original pool becomes SWEEP/RECLAIM -> REVERSAL WATCH/FAVORED.
- Before a sweep, user-facing text is CONTINUATION LEAN / REVERSAL LEAN, reserving FAVORED for observed
  post-breach behavior.

4) Market Intelligence / Move Radar
- Early-direction overlay remains shadow-only and has zero One-Brain decision weight.
- Move Radar upgrades quickly on real pressure but downgrades more slowly to reduce one-snapshot flicker.
- Opposite-to-15m early pressure is shown as AMBER reversal/countertrend caution rather than a full green/red
  trend confirmation.
- Evidence freshness now discounts aged feed components.

5) Recording / validation
- Market Intelligence export includes early_direction, dominant_context, direction_context, fast-family count,
  sweep acceptance state, sweep age and locked sweep-anchor zone.
- Day Memory records raw vs display-stable One-Brain scores and option/participation quality.
