Nifty Seller Lite 2.72.0 — Institutional Opportunity Window + Shadow Alerts

PURPOSE
- Adds a zero-weight Institutional Opportunity Window inside Market Intelligence.
- It does NOT identify a real institution, hidden order, trader identity, stop placement or private intent.
- It asks whether public-market conditions look unusually attractive for large directional participation.

SIX CORE GATES
1. Directional Edge
2. Opposition Weakness
3. Path Clearance
4. Participation Capacity Proxy
5. Trigger Readiness
6. Pressure Effectiveness

Window states: CLOSED -> FORMING -> OPEN -> STRONG.
OPEN requires all 6/6 gates plus live core-feed/data-safety checks. There is no extra persistence wait after 6/6 is reached.
W/M and strong candles remain supportive only and never mandatory.

ALERTS
- Telegram/UI alert on first OPEN transition.
- Optional material upgrade alert on OPEN -> STRONG.
- Existing Smart Alert controller remains the single delivery voice; no parallel Telegram subsystem.
- Existing critical risk/conflict alerts keep higher priority where appropriate.

RESEARCH RATIONALE
The design separates directional edge from execution/liquidity conditions because market-microstructure evidence shows that order imbalance can affect prices while liquidity and execution costs materially determine how efficiently large orders can be expressed. Participation Capacity is therefore explicitly labelled a proxy rather than a claim about private institutional depth.
