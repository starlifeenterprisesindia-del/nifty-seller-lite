ONE BRAIN / NIFTY SELLER — v2.67.0_MI_LIQUIDITY_MOVE_RADAR_SHADOW

Purpose
- Extend the existing shadow-only Market Intelligence Engine without changing the canonical One Brain decision path.
- Add probable liquidity/stop-cascade mapping and a simple always-visible Move Radar for large-move precaution.

New shadow intelligence
- Probable Liquidity Map from already-available levels, barriers, swings, session levels, option walls, patterns and modest round-number context.
- Upside / Downside Hunt Pressure, Hunt Bias and Hunt Strength.
- Primary Liquidity Target plus optional Extension Zone.
- Reach Score / Reach State and Path Clearance.
- Prior-target sweep/breach detection with Continuation Favored / Reversal Favored / Unclear outcome.
- MOVE RADAR: NORMAL / WATCH / BULLISH MOVE BUILDING / BEARISH MOVE BUILDING / BIG MOVE WATCH / strong build-up states.
- Lightweight blink/pulse UI for green bullish, red bearish and amber direction-unclear precaution states.
- Combined alert candidates for Big Move Precaution, Liquidity Hunt Watch and Liquidity Sweep/Breach, integrated with existing alignment/conflict/build-up alerts.
- 90-second routine-alert cooldown plus state/fingerprint deduplication; critical conflict/sweep/alignment state changes can bypass routine cooldown.

UI simplification
- Main Market Intelligence card now shows one short story: MARKET, MOVE, LIQUIDITY TARGET, HUNT, AFTER TARGET, MOVE PRESSURE, ONE BRAIN, STATUS, and 5m/15m/30m outlook.
- Detailed scores and expert evidence remain hidden under Advanced / Research detail.

Validation downloads
- Existing v2.66.1 Market Intelligence Test Pack retained.
- Added liquidity_hunt_review.csv and liquidity/radar/sweep fields to snapshot + alert exports.

Golden-rule protection
- Zero new broker/API calls.
- Zero Market Intelligence decision weight in canonical One Brain.
- No imports added to decision.py, simple_brain.py, future_brain.py, execution_guard.py, trade_plan.py, position_guardian.py or snapshot_service.py.
- Liquidity subsystem consumes already-fused directional/expansion scores; it does not create a second directional vote or feed back into common evidence.
- Missing evidence remains NO VOTE.
- All target/hunt/reach values are evidence scores, not calibrated probabilities and not claims of exact retail stop money or participant intent.
